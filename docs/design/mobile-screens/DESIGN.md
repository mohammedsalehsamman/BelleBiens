## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a romantic, sensory, and culturally rooted identity—embodying an artisanal floral atelier, French-Levantine patisserie, and bespoke gifting destination in Damascus. The visual mood reconciles historic Old Damascus warmth (limestone courtyards, hand-pressed botanical paper, Damask roses) with contemporary Parisian salon refinement.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Tactile Warmth & Editorial Romance**: Natural ivory surfaces replace harsh digital whites, evoking cotton rag paper and linen ribbons. High-contrast typography pairs classical serif gestures with clean, highly readable Arabic typographic craft.
- **RTL-Native Poetics**: Visual priority begins naturally from the right, honouring traditional calligraphic flow, while maintaining precise symmetry and seamless balance for bilingual visitors.
- **Tone & Atmosphere**: Fragrant, hospitable, sophisticated, and deeply personal. Interfaces must never feel like generic e-commerce; they should evoke stepping through an arched jasmine-draped courtyard into an exclusive boutique café.

## Colors

The color palette translates the natural pigments of Damask roses (*Rosa damascena*), olive foliage, and sunlit limestone walls into an accessible, high-contrast digital environment.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#872341` / Deep Velvet Bordeaux)**: The anchor of luxury and botanical passion. Used for primary CTAs, active states, key titles, and brand insignia. Contrast against cream backgrounds is exceptionally sharp (AA/AAA compliant).
- **Secondary (`#E8B4B8` / Rose Petal Blush)**: Soft, welcoming touchpoint for secondary accents, decorative badges, active container highlights, and delicate wash overlays.
- **Tertiary (`#8F9E8B` / Damascus Sage)**: Reflects botanical stems and freshness. Used for organic categories, freshness tags, seasonal badges, and positive micro-moments.
- **Neutral Dark (`#2C2422` / Roasted Espresso)**: A warm-tinted alternative to sterile black. Delivers humanistic contrast for body copy, data tables, and dense Arabic typography without visual fatigue.
- **Backgrounds & Canvas (`#FAF6F0` Warm Alabaster to `#FFFFFF` Pure Surface)**: Creates an organic, tactile depth where cards stand distinctively without heavy structural styling.
- **Semantic Colors**:
  - Success / Active Order: `#2D7A58` (Forest Emerald)
  - Pending / Preparation: `#C9862C` (Warm Amber Honey)
  - Critical / Sold Out: `#A62B32` (Crimson Carmine)
  - Surface Border: `#EFE8E1` (Whisper Parchment)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy harmonizes Arabic and Latin scripts across two linguistic cadences:
- **Headlines & Editorial Statements**: Set in **Playfair Display** for brand titles, hero quotes, and Latin boutique typography, conveying bespoke heritage. In purely Arabic editorial headlines, apply a heavier weight of **IBM Plex Sans** (600 or 700) with expanded line-height to allow diacritics and ascenders natural breathing room.
- **Reading & Product UI**: Set in **IBM Plex Sans** (with full Arabic glyph coverage). The font’s open counters, balanced baseline, and humanist terminal curves offer pristine legibility in complex RTL menus, ingredient specifications, and inventory tables.
- **Numerals & Currency**: By default, format prices and quantities with clean Western numerals (`1, 2, 3...`) accompanied by native localized currency denotations (`ل.س` for Syrian Pounds, or `$` for international boutique orders). Numerals must always remain tabular (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) inside checkout ledgers and POS dashboards.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an adaptable 8pt spatial grid system configured for graceful responsiveness from mobile storefronts to multi-column administrative back-offices.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 640px)**: 4-column layout, 16px margins, 12px gutters. Emphasizes edge-to-edge organic visual cards, quick thumbnail tap targets, and sticky bottom utility bars.
- **Tablet (640px – 1024px)**: 8-column layout, 24px margins, 16px gutters. Suited for split-view custom bouquet builders and café floor order managers.
- **Desktop & POS Workspace (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout, 40px outer margins, 24px gutters, max-width constrained to 1440px for customer-facing interfaces; fluid 100% width with a fixed 280px navigation sidebar for admin dashboards.

### Flow & Alignment (RTL First)
- All structural margins, padding insets, and visual anchors originate right-to-left. 
- Elements mirroring across LTR: Chevron indicators, catalog sorting trees, and back actions mirror strictly; universal icons (camera, shopping bag, search) retain standard axis.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than synthetic dark drop-shadows, this design system achieves spatial hierarchy through delicate tonal surfaces, warm ambient illumination, and hairline borders.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#FAF6F0` (Warm Alabaster). The foundation of the entire app experience.
- **Tier 1 (Resting Cards & Panels)**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Porcelain) combined with a 1px solid border in `#EFE8E1`. Elevation is supported by an ambient drop shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(135, 35, 65, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Controls & Navigation)**: Bottom navigation bars, cart sheets, and popovers utilize `#FFFFFF` with a soft rose blur tint: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(44, 36, 34, 0.08), 0 2px 6px 0 rgba(135, 35, 65, 0.03)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Toast Alerts)**: High-altitude elements surrounded by a gentle backdrop blur overlay (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(44, 36, 34, 0.35)`).

## Shapes

The geometric personality mirrors hand-cut pastry cartons, rounded marble arches, and smoothed glassware found in the Damascus tea room.

- **Base Radius (`rounded-md`, 8px)**: Reserved for micro-components, text inputs, quantity pickers, and table row selections.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg`, 16px)**: Applied to product cards, pastry showcases, order status panels, and dialogue frames.
- **Feature Radius (`rounded-xl`, 24px)**: Applied to hero banners, story carousels, and bottom bottom-sheet drawers.
- **Full Radius (`rounded-full`, 9999px)**: Applied to boutique pill buttons, category filter tabs, status pills, and floating action buttons (FABs).

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Calls to Action
- **Primary ("طلب مخصص / أضف إلى السلة")**: Solid `#872341` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-full` curvature. Minimum height of 48px on mobile for effortless touch ergonomics. On hover/active, transitions to `#6E1B32` with a subtle inward glow.
- **Secondary ("استعراض القائمة")**: 1.5px border in `#872341`, background transparent, text `#872341`. Soft `#F7D6D8` tint fill on interaction.
- **Tertiary / Botanical Action ("صمّم باقتك")**: Pill-shaped button featuring a gentle gradient fill from `#872341` to `#A23152`, adorned with a miniature rose or ribbon icon prefix.

### 2. Category & Filter Chips
- Capsule-shaped pills (`rounded-full`, padding: 8px 16px).
- **Default State**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `#EFE8E1`, text `#2C2422`.
- **Selected State**: Background `#872341`, border `#872341`, text `#FFFFFF`, accompanied by an organic soft transition.

### 3. Cards (Floral, Patisserie & Gift Boxes)
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, 16px border-radius, and 1px border `#EFE8E1`.
- Image aspect ratio of 4:5 for artisanal bouquets and gift hampers, 1:1 for bakery items.
- Incorporates a floating top-right badge for origin or freshness (e.g., `ورد جوري دمشقي قطاف اليوم` in `#8F9E8B` sage green badge with white text).
- Price display highlighted in bold `#872341` typography with clear currency formatting (`ل.س`).

### 4. Input Fields & Form Controls
- Inputs use a warm tinted background (`#FFFFFF`), 1px outline in `#EFE8E1`, with 12px internal padding and 8px border-radius.
- Focus state activates an outline in `#872341` with a 3px diffused focus-ring in `rgba(135, 35, 65, 0.12)`.
- Labels are firmly anchored to the right for RTL flow, featuring a small botanical glyph as required-field indicators.

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Circular radio buttons for single-choice gift packaging options (e.g., "تغليف مخملي فاخر", "صندوق هدايا خشبي محفور").
- Selected indicator utilizes an inner dot in `#872341` surrounded by a soft blush ring (`#E8B4B8`).

### 6. Mobile Bottom Navigation & Floating Cart Bar
- Fixed bottom bar with blurred alabaster background (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.94)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).
- Center-weighted prominence for the custom bouquet builder ("صمّم باقتك") using an elevated primary circle button.
- Cart status displays as a floating capsule above navigation with item count, live summary, and smooth spring animation on item addition.

### 7. Admin Dashboard & Atelier Management
- **Sidebar**: Clean, structured vertical navigation with active states indicated by a right-aligned vertical bar in `#872341` and soft rose surface highlights.
- **Status Badges**:
  - `قيد التحضير` (Preparing): Soft amber background (`#FDF3E3`), text `#C9862C`.
  - `جاهز للتوصيل` (Ready for Dispatch): Soft emerald background (`#E6F4ED`), text `#2D7A58`.
  - `مكتمل` (Completed): Light slate background (`#F0EFEB`), text `#605856`.
- **Data Tables**: Minimalist row separators with 1px border `#EFE8E1`, alternating hover rows in `#FAF6F0`, and right-aligned typographic alignment optimized for Arabic administrative clarity.
